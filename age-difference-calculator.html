What is an Age Difference Calculator?

An Age Difference Calculator helps you find the exact age gap between two people. Simply enter both dates of birth and the calculator will show the difference in years, months and days.

How to Use

Enter the date of birth of the first person, enter the date of birth of the second person, and click the Calculate Age Difference button.
